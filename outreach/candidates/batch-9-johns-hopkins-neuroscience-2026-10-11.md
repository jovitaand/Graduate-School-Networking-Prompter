- **Title:** Bloomberg Assistant Professor of Neuroscience; Assistant Professor of Neurology and Genetic Medicine; Terkowitz Family Rising Professor, Brain Science Institute; Associate Director, JHU Single-cell Training and Analysis Center.
- **Location:** Baltimore, MD (Eastern).

## 4. Sangwon Kim, PhD (Tier 3)
- **Title:** Associate Professor of Medicine, Division of Endocrinology, Diabetes and Metabolism, Johns Hopkins Bayview.
- **Location:** Baltimore, MD (Eastern; Bayview campus).
- **Email:** skim132@jhmi.edu (Kim's JHU Neuroscience faculty page, `neuroscience.jhu.edu/research/faculty/137`).
