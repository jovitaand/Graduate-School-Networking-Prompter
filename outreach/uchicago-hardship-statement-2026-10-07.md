# UChicago Social Sciences fee waiver: Category E hardship statement (drafted 2026-10-07)

For: Psychology PhD, Computational Cognitive Neuroscience (fee $90). **Goes inside the application, not by email.** The Division says: "Please do not contact admissions offices or faculty to request a code."

Rules from the official page (read 2026-10-07): a written statement explaining the hardship, plus English-language documents that show the full legal name and are dated within the last 12 months. Accepted documents:
- Bank statements from the previous 6 months showing deposits and balances, with a US dollar equivalent (redact the account number).
- Employer verification of current income (paystub or employer letter).
- Government or municipal proof of social welfare assistance.
- GRE Fee Waiver/Fee Reduction certificate.

The faster route is a waiver code from a Social Sciences admissions webinar (Category D).

I am an international applicant living and working in Abu Dhabi, United Arab Emirates. Due to the geopolitical situation in the region where I am located, I would humbly request a fee waiver. [OPTIONAL, ONLY IF TRUE: add one or two sentences on how the situation affects you personally, for example on income, banking or international payments.]

I have attached [bank statements from the last six months with US dollar equivalents / a letter from my employer verifying my income], as required. [Account numbers are redacted.]

I recently completed my MS in Applied Data Science at Syracuse University, and I currently work as a Biostatistician at NYU Abu Dhabi's Center for Brain and Health. Thank you for your time.

Sincerely,
Jovita Andrews

## Before submitting
- Use your full legal name, as on your documents. Documents must be dated within the last 12 months and in English (certified translation if not).
- Do not state anything about your finances that your documents do not show. The two bracketed sentences are yours to fill in or remove.
