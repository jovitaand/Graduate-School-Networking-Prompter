# Penn Neuroscience Graduate Group: application fee (checked 2026-10-08)

**No fee waiver is needed. Penn BGS charges no application fee.** Official BGS Admissions FAQ (med.upenn.edu/bgs/admissions-faqs.html): "Is there an application fee? There is no fee to apply to BGS." The Neuroscience Graduate Group (NGG) applies through the BGS online application (apply.med.upenn.edu).

- Application period: October 1 to December 1 (BGS Admissions page). Interviews January and February; notifications mid-January to early March.
- NGG requires a CV.
- Funding: all NGG students receive fellowships covering tuition, fees and stipend ($49,000 for 2026-27, per the BGS Student Funding page).
