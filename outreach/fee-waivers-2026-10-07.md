# Application fee waiver requests (researched 2026-10-07)

Status: **Two Gmail drafts created (Emory, UCSB). NOT SENT.** No email goes out without Jovita's explicit YES. Stated reason, as requested by Jovita: geopolitical situation where she is located (Abu Dhabi).

Each school's official page was read on 2026-10-07. The fee waiver rules differ a lot, and for three of the five an email is the wrong channel.

| Program | Official rule for international applicants | Email drafted? |
|---|---|---|
| Emory Laney Graduate School, Neuroscience PhD | **Eligible.** Financial hardship waiver is requested inside the application (link on the Signature page), with uploaded documentation. The page lists "currency rates or other economic conditions" as acceptable for international applicants. Review takes 5 business days. | Yes: `lgsadmissions@emory.edu` (draft r1730531187851638429). Says she will also file the in-application request. |
| UC Santa Barbara, Dynamical Neuroscience PhD | **Not eligible.** "International applicants are not eligible for fee waivers." Questions go to `gradadmissions@graddiv.ucsb.edu`. Waiver must be requested at least one week before the Dec 1 deadline. | Yes, worded as a question about whether any exception exists (draft r8755744113796327970). Likely answer is no. |
| UChicago, Psychology PhD (Computational Cognitive Neuroscience) | Handled by the Division of the Social Sciences. Category E (financial hardship) takes a written statement plus English-language documents dated within 12 months (bank statements, employer income letter, etc.). The page says **"Please do not contact admissions offices or faculty to request a code."** Waiver codes come from attending an admissions webinar. | No. Use the webinar code or the Category E statement inside the application. |
| UCSF, Biological and Medical Informatics PhD | **Not eligible.** "International applicants are not eligible to apply for fee waivers." Also: "Applicants should not email individuals within UCSF to request an application fee waiver." | No. |
| ASU, Neuroscience PhD (School of Life Sciences) | The SoLS Graduate Office **cannot waive the fee**. A faculty member may pay it on the applicant's behalf, after the applicant has emailed faculty with shared research interests. | No. The email would go to a faculty member, not the graduate office. |

Fees (for reference): Emory $75; UChicago PhD $90; UCSF $140 international; ASU $115 or $120 on a nonimmigrant visa in the U.S., $70 or $75 otherwise.

Sources: gs.emory.edu/admissions/fee-waivers.html; graddiv.ucsb.edu fee waiver page; socialsciences.uchicago.edu/admissions/application-materials/application-fee; graduate.ucsf.edu/admission/application-fee-waivers; sols.asu.edu/admission/graduate/faq.
